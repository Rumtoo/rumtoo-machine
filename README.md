## 🏢 About Rumtoo Machinery

**Rumtoo Machinery** is a world-class manufacturer specializing in high-performance **plastic recycling machinery, washing lines, shredders, crushers, and pelletizing systems**. ## 🛠️ Core Turnkey Solutions

### 1. Plastic Washing & Recycling Lines
Turnkey washing plants designed to maximize flake purity, minimize water & energy consumption, and ensure stable 24/7 continuous operation.

* **PET Bottle Recycling Washing Line**:
  * Specialized for post-consumer beverage bottles, thermoforms, and sheets.
  * Includes automated de-labeling, wet granulating, dynamic hot washing, triple-stage sink-float separation, and centrifugal dewatering.
  * Produces food-grade (bottle-to-bottle) and fiber-grade PET flakes (< 50 ppm PVC contamination).
* **PP / PE Film & Woven Bag Washing Line**:
  * Engineered for agricultural films, LDPE stretch films, post-consumer garbage bags, and PP raffia jumbo sacks (FIBC).
  * High-friction washing units strip away soil, grease, inks, and glue.
  * Equipped with advanced film squeezer dryers reducing moisture to under 1-3%.
* **Rigid Plastic (HDPE / PP / PVC) Washing Line**:
  * Handles hollow bottles, shampoo/oil drums, battery cases, crates, and hard scrap.
  * Heavy-duty sink-float tanks sort light plastics (PP/PE) from heavy sinks (PVC/PET/metals).

### 2. Size Reduction (Shredders & Crushers)
Built with unstoppable torque, low rotor speeds, and wear-resistant cutting geometries to conquer the toughest plastic scrap.

* **Single-Shaft Shredders (Hydraulic Ram / Drawer Push)**:
  * High-torque reduction for baled films, woven sacks, solid plastic purge lumps, thick-wall pipes, and solid blocks without jamming.
* **Double-Shaft Shredders (Low-Speed, High-Torque)**:
  * Hexagonal rotor shafts with intermeshing shear blades; perfect for municipal solid waste (MSW), car bumpers, drums, electronic scrap, and rubber tires.
* **Heavy-Duty Plastic Crushers & Wet Granulators**:
  * Precision V-shaped, staggered, and claw rotor designs for uniform flake size with minimal fine dust.
  * Water-injected wet granulating prevents blade overheating and pre-cleans materials.
* **Specialized HDPE / PVC Pipe Shredders**:
  * Horizontal feeding troughs for long-length, large-diameter pipes (up to 1200 mm diameter) without pre-cutting.

### 3. Plastic Pelletizing Systems
Convert cleaned plastic flakes, regrinds, or edge trims into uniform, degassed, high-density granules for injection molding, blowing, or extrusion.

* **Cutter-Compactor Plastic Film Pelletizing Line (PP/PE)**:
  * Integrates cutting, pre-heating, compacting, and single-screw extrusion in one compact footprint.
  * Powerful double-vacuum degassing ports eliminate trapped volatile gases and moisture.
* **Rigid Plastic Extrusion & Pelletizing Lines**:
  * Single or two-stage (cascade) extruders with double-piston hydraulic continuous screen changers to filter residual micro-impurities without stopping production.
* **Water-Ring, Strand & Under-Water Pelletizers**:
  * Die-face water-ring cutting with high-speed pneumatic blade adjustments ensures uniform, bubble-free plastic pellets.
* **EPS Foam Pelletizing Line**:
  * Compact densification and pelletizing systems specifically optimized for expanded polystyrene (Styrofoam) foam scrap.

### 4. Auxiliary & Separation Equipment
* **Drying Systems**: Centrifugal Dewatering Dryers, Mechanical Film Squeezers, and Thermal Pipe Drying Systems.
* **Sorting & Separation**: Zig-Zag Air Classifiers (label and dust removal), Eddy Current Separators (non-ferrous metal recovery), and Over-Belt Magnetic Cross Separators.
* **Baler Machines**: Manual vertical balers for compact spaces and fully automatic horizontal hydraulic balers with auto-tie for high-volume OCC, cartons, and plastic baling.
* **Wear Parts & Tooling**: D2 / SKD-11 / Hardox rotor and stator blades, screens, and automatic knife sharpening machines.
