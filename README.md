## 🏢 About Rumtoo Machinery

**[Rumtoo Machinery](https://www.recyclemachine.net/)** is an internationally certified manufacturer specializing in high-performance **[Plastic Recycling Machinery](https://www.recyclemachine.net/recycling-machines/)**, **[Washing Lines](https://www.recyclemachine.net/recycling-washing-systems/)**, **[Plastic Shredders](https://www.recyclemachine.net/plastic-shredders/)**, **[Plastic Crushers](https://www.recyclemachine.net/plastic-crusher/)**, and **[Plastic Pelletizers](https://www.recyclemachine.net/plastic-pelletizers/)**. Headquartered in the plastics machinery industrial hub of Zhangjiagang (Suzhou City, Jiangsu Province, China), Rumtoo brings over two decades of engineering excellence to recyclers in 50+ countries worldwide.

We believe that **plastic is not waste — it is a valuable circular resource**. Unlike standard equipment builders, Rumtoo specializes in processing **heavily soiled, post-consumer, and challenging industrial plastic waste**, converting contaminated scrap into high-purity flakes and premium recycled pellets that meet stringent international manufacturing standards.

## 🔄 Turnkey Project Workflow

We provide full-lifecycle engineering support from plant layout to installation:

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A["1. Feedstock Analysis & Consultation"] --> B["2. Custom CAD Layout Planning"]
    B --> C["3. Precision Manufacturing (30-90 Days)"]
    C --> D["4. Pre-Shipment Factory Trial Run"]
    D --> E["5. Global Containerized Shipping"]
    E --> F["6. On-Site Installation & Training"]
    F --> G["7. Lifetime Support & Spare Parts Supply"]
```

1. **[Feedstock Consultation](https://www.recyclemachine.net/contact-us/)**: We analyze incoming scrap contamination levels, utility constraints, and target output purity.
2. **CAD Workshop Layout Engineering**: Custom CAD layouts engineered specifically for your factory dimensions, water circulation, and power infrastructure.
3. **Precision Manufacturing**: Heavy-duty CNC machining, reinforced chassis welding, and premium global components (Siemens, Schneider, NSK/SKF, ABB).
4. **Pre-Shipment Trial Run**: Full electrical and mechanical dry/wet trial testing in our workshop. Clients are welcome to inspect in person or via live HD video conference.
5. **Global Export Shipping**: Anti-rust treatment, wooden crates, and secure containerization.
6. **On-Site Installation & Training**: Certified commissioning engineers deploy to your site for 7–14 days to install, commission, and train local operators.
7. **Comprehensive 1-Year Warranty & 24/7 Technical Support**: Swift global spare parts dispatch and lifetime remote assistance. Learn more about our **[Ordering Process](https://www.recyclemachine.net/ordering-process/)**.
